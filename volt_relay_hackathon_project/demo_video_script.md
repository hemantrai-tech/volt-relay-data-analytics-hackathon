# 60-second demo video narration

**0–6s:** “I analysed a simulated battery-swapping network with 3.88 million attempts across six Indian cities.” Show title and KPI cards.

**6–18s:** “Overall completion was 94%. I separate technical failures, queue abandonment and rider cancellations because each needs a different response.” Show city chart.

**18–30s:** “No charged battery accounted for 92% of technical failures. Jaipur and Delhi NCR show the highest observed failure rates, so I would start a station-hour availability investigation there.”

**30–40s:** “Heat-alert days had a higher failure rate, but that is an association. I would compare like-for-like stations and hours and test a heat-readiness playbook.”

**40–51s:** “The cost fields omit depreciation and labour. The 30-day repeat proxy is saturated. Neither supports a confident margin or retention conclusion.”

**51–60s:** “My recommendations: targeted availability checks, battery-health audits, better rider cohorts and complete unit economics. Use data to guide a test, and be clear about what it cannot prove.” Show recommendations.

## Recording and Drive sharing
1. Open `index.html` in Chrome. Record the screen with Windows Snipping Tool screen recording or Xbox Game Bar (`Win+Alt+R`). Scroll slowly and read the narration.
2. Save and replay the MP4 to check picture and sound.
3. Upload it to Google Drive. Set General access to **Anyone with the link — Viewer**, then verify it opens in an incognito window.
