06 / Retention

Repair the measure

Constructed 30-day activation/repeat is near 100%, indicating a ceiling or instrumentation issue. It cannot establish healthy retention or a decline.

Use eligible 30/60/90-day signup cohorts and month-to-month active rider retention.

Recommendations / next 30 days

Test the operating levers

  1. Availability sprint: start with Jaipur and Delhi NCR; reconcile failed attempts, station-hour telemetry and charging queues.
  2. Heat readiness: stage charged inventory and monitor turnaround; compare matched stations/hours before attributing impact.
  3. Battery audit: inspect batteries below 80% SOH by model, station and tenure before changing retirement policy.
  4. Fix measurement: add cohort retention and battery depreciation/labour to station economics before expansion decisions.
Scope and quality

Evidence limits

Associations guide tests

City, weather, expansion, tariff and battery comparisons are descriptive. Mix and simulation rules may explain differences. The station-hour stockout proxy does not confirm rider encounters.
